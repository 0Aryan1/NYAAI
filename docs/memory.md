## Build decisions (with reasons)
- **2026-11-22, official judgments come from a public mirror of e-SCR, not e-SCR itself.** The Supreme Court's own search (scr.sci.gov.in, sci.gov.in) needs a captcha; the loader never gets around one. The AWS Open Data dataset "Indian Supreme Court Judgments" (CC-BY-4.0) holds e-SCR's own PDFs and its result pages, one folder per year, updated weekly (last 2026-11-18). `services/escr` reads `metadata/tar/year=YYYY/metadata.tar` (no parquet: adding a dependency was refused, and the tar is plain), keeps a small index per year in our storage (`escr/catalog/YYYY.json`, recent years refreshed weekly), and fetches `data/pdf/year=YYYY/english/<key>_EN.pdf`. Each copy records its link (the mirror) and the official citations ("[2014] 13 S.C.R. 895; 2014 INSC 809"). Attribution for CC-BY is the link plus this note; add it to the sources page before launch.
- **2026-11-22, e-SCR PDFs before 2018 are scans of the printed report** (a picture per page plus machine-read text: "set qside", "gnJunds", margin letters A–H inside lines). Their words are worse than Kanoon's, and a quoted span must match the source word for word, so a scan never replaces cleaner text: it is kept in `source_copies` (in_use = false) with how far the two agree (69–97%), and its citations go on the case. From 2018 the PDFs are typeset and their text replaces Kanoon's. A scan is used as the text only when no other copy exists, and then the source is `scanned` and provisional ("check quotes against the PDF"). Detected from pdf.js image operators on sampled pages (`pdfIsScanned`).
- **2026-11-22, replacing a Kanoon copy keeps the same source row and, where the paragraph is the same, the same chunk id and role label** (`matchChunks`: 4-word runs, ≥ 80% of the shorter text, comparable length; "same" at ≥ 90%). Citations, concept links and the Studio drawer keep working; only changed text is re-embedded and only new paragraphs labelled. The official copy must contain ≥ 60% of the text in use (`SAME_JUDGMENT`), or nothing changes (`mismatch`). "Labels checked" is cleared unless every label carried over. The Kanoon raw JSON stays in storage until its expiry rule (not yet enforced) removes it.
- **2026-11-22, what the official copies fixed:** Cox and Kings (2023) from Kanoon held only Narasimha J.'s concurrence (58 paras), labelled "majority"; the official copy has the CJI's majority opinion too (169 paras) and types them majority + concurring. Gayatri Balasamy (2025) was all "majority" with no judge, the dissent included; now majority (Khanna CJI) + dissent (Viswanathan J.). Vidya Drolia (2020) gains Ramana J.'s concurrence; PASL Wind (2021) gets the court's own numbering (1, 2, 3.1–3.7, 4.1–4.8, …) instead of ours.
- **2026-11-22, PDF judgment reader (`pdfToJudgment`).** Lines come from pdf.js with font size and left edge (`pdfLines`); superscript footnote markers are taken out of the text ("1996¹" would read "19961") and the notes they mark are dropped from the foot of the page (the PDF keeps them). Also dropped: running heads (matched anywhere on the page; shortened words like "ENG.", "AUTH." count), page numbers (also inside a line), margin letters, the author's name in footers, editor's notes, tables of contents, signature stamps, and the next case that e-SCR prints after the last page. The court's text starts at the earliest marker in the first 60% ("JUDGMENT / ORDER OF THE SUPREME COURT", a "Judgment(s) … was/were delivered" sentence even when broken over lines, "J U D G M E N T"); before it is the headnote (reporter's summary only, case details and counsel lists cut, ≤ 12,000 characters). Hyphens at line ends are dropped only when the joined word is used elsewhere and the hyphenated form is not. Opinions: e-SCR's reporter notes ("Ed. Note: … pronounced judgment on behalf of …", "(per X, J.) (Dissenting)", "Per X, J. (Concurring)") type the opinions when the text does not; the one opinion left beside named dissents and concurrences is the majority when dissenters are fewer than half the Bench.
- **2026-11-22, splitter: sub-paragraph numbers ("3.1.", "4.2") are the court's numbering** (first part of the next paragraph, or next part of the current one; a quoted clause "6.1" inside para 3 is not), and "NAME, CJI" starts an opinion. For PDFs only (`quotesMarked`), the numbering share counts the court's own unquoted blocks and a run of 25+ numbers counts; Kanoon's blockquote flag is unreliable, and applying this to Kanoon text merged Associate Builders from 106 paragraphs to 36 (caught by `resplit`, reverted).
- **2026-11-22, editor uploads go through the API** (`POST /library/uploads/judgment`, PDF as the body, details in the query string), not the LLD's presigned browser upload: library editors only, 50 MB cap, `%PDF-` checked, and SeaweedFS would need CORS for a direct upload. The job carries the source id, the storage key (a content hash) and two flags only. "From an official court website" → origin `court_site`, live once read (unless scanned); otherwise `upload`, provisional. "It replaces" swaps the text of a held judgment only if it is the same judgment.
